Omnatix

Every one of these found money the business already had.

Four demonstrations across different industries. Each starts from the kind of raw exports a South African business already produces, and ends with a decision someone can act on this week. Open any of them and the dashboard will find its own problem in front of you.

Every business here is fictional and every figure is generated. The findings are planted on purpose, because a demo that only displays numbers proves nothing.

From raw files

CSV exports land as they are. Nothing is corrected by hand. Mixed date formats, duplicate rows, missing costs and orphan product codes are all resolved in the pipeline, visibly.

Tested every build

Bronze, silver and gold layers in dbt, with a test suite that runs on every refresh. A failing test stops the publish, so a number nobody checked never reaches a screen.

No server

Each dashboard runs its own query engine in your browser. There is no backend and no database to host, which is why these cost nothing to keep online.

Honest about age

Every dashboard states how current it is, measured against your clock rather than baked in when it was built. Most dashboards do not admit this, and several are quietly stale.

Omnatix builds the pipeline as well as the dashboard. Figures in rand, excluding VAT.

All data on these pages is synthetic. No client information appears anywhere in this repository or on these demos.
