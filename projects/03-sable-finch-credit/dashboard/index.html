Synthetic data Sable & Finch Credit is a fictional lender. Every figure in this pack is generated and contains deliberately planted findings. No real company or borrower data appears here.

Built by Omnatix. Loan, repayment, arrears and collections exports land as CSV, are modelled through bronze, silver and gold layers in dbt on DuckDB, tested on every build, and published as Parquet. This page runs SQL against those files in your browser using DuckDB-WASM. There is no backend and no database server.

The recommendations and draft resolutions are written by a language model from the same gold tables, after every rebuild, and each names the model that wrote it. They are drafts for the committee to test against the figures, not decisions.
