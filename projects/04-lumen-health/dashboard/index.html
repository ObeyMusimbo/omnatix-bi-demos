Synthetic data Lumen Health Network is a fictional practice group. Every figure on this page is generated and contains deliberately planted findings. There are no patient identifiers anywhere in this dataset: no names, no dates of birth and no identity numbers. A patient is a reference number, an age band and a scheme.

Built by Omnatix. Diary, billing, claims and reception exports land as CSV, are modelled through bronze, silver and gold layers in dbt on DuckDB, tested on every build, and published as Parquet. This page loads those files and runs SQL against them in your browser using DuckDB-WASM. There is no backend and no database server.

Money figures are in rand and exclude VAT. Claim settlement is measured from submission to payment rather than from date of service, so a slow scheme and a slow practice stay separate numbers. Nothing on this page is clinical or financial advice.
