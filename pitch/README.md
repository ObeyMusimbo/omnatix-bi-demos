## The thirty second version

For the lift, the WhatsApp voice note, or the first line of a cold email:

> "Omnatix finds the money a business is already losing. We take the exports a company already
> has, sales, fleet, loans or claims, build a tested warehouse from them, and in three weeks
> show the leaks nobody's reports can see, each with a rand value and an owner. Then an AI
> analyst checks it every night so the money stays found. A distributor we modelled grew revenue
> 12% and gross profit 4%, and we could show exactly where the R12.6 million went."

| Clinics, practices, healthcare groups | Lumen | R17.4m billed for care already delivered, never collected |
| An investor or a generalist | Kestrel, then the landing page | The map, then "four industries, same method" |

## Every meeting

- Load the demo once before the meeting, on your own laptop.
- One demo for a buyer. The landing page and the range are for investors.
- Open on the gap, stop talking, and let them ask.
- Point at the AI panel's actions and owner, not only the number.
- Leave with a date, not a promise to follow up.
